Yinka, PMHNP-BC

Yinka is a Board-Certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC) dedicated to supporting the mental and emotional well-being of individuals and families. She provides compassionate, patient-centered care through psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, medication management, and supportive therapy, using evidence-based approaches to help patients navigate life transitions, emotional challenges, and mental health conditions. She’s committed to creating a supportive environment where patients feel heard, respected, and empowered in their mental health journey.

She holds a Master’s degree in Clinical Nurse Leader from Seton Hall University, where she developed strong leadership, evidence-based practice, and care coordination skills that continue to guide her clinical practice. Yinka is committed to holistic care that recognizes the strong connection between mental, emotional, and overall wellness.

🌿 Yinka’s mission is to nurture and empower families, ensuring every stage of life — from birth to parenthood — is supported with compassion, balance, and holistic well-being.
